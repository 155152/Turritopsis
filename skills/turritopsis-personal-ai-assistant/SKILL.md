# Turritopsis Personal AI Assistant

Map the assistant as a relationship with explicit authority, not as a persona plus tools.

1. Use `$turritopsis-onboarding` for local scan and validated application.
2. Read [stage-suite.md](references/stage-suite.md).
3. Define the assistant's role, non-role, human owner, channels, and action surface before describing personality.
4. Separate human consent and direction, remembered preferences, runtime evidence, model inference, and external-service state.
5. Give every proactive or consequential action an explicit authority level, confirmation rule, visible result, and failure behavior.
6. Preserve warmth and continuity without treating intimacy, repetition, or confidence as permission.
7. Keep user-facing explanations distinct from private internal scheduling, ranking, or safety inputs.
8. Use [fixture.json](references/fixture.json) only as a fictional quality example.
